## Scope

These are **course-completion certificates**: they record finished coursework in the Internet
Society's learning tracks and are not professional certifications, accreditations, or a claim of
competence in the subject beyond that.

Nineteen certificates were issued on 27 October 2026. The wallet also carries ten lesson badges
(five Encryption lessons, four IXP lessons, and one Internet Impact Brief lesson); those are listed
here only as context and are not certificates.

Every credential ID above is the Accredible credential identifier and resolves at
`https://certificates.isoc.org/<id>`. The same nineteen appear on
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sushant-poudel2028/details/certifications/).
